# BX-3000 Ultra Lithium Battery

**Product Name**: BX-3000 Ultra Lithium  
**Manufacturer**: TechPower Industries  
**Model**: BX-3000  

## Overview

The BX-3000 is a premium rechargeable lithium-ion battery designed for high-performance portable electronics. It combines cutting-edge battery chemistry with intelligent power management to deliver reliable, long-lasting energy for demanding applications.

## Technical Specifications

**Energy & Capacity**
- Nominal Capacity: 5000 mAh
- Rated Energy: 18.5 Wh
- Minimum Capacity: 4900 mAh (after 100 charge cycles)

**Voltage Characteristics**
- Nominal Voltage: 3.7V
- Maximum Voltage: 4.2V
- Minimum Voltage: 2.5V
- Average Operating Voltage: 3.6V

**Physical Specifications**
- Dimensions: 85 mm × 65 mm × 12 mm
- Weight: 185 grams
- Casing Material: Aluminum alloy with protective coating

**Performance**
- Fast Charging: Supports up to 2A charging current (0-100% in ~2.5 hours)
- Standard Charging: 1A charging current (0-100% in ~5 hours)
- Maximum Discharge Current: 3.5A continuous
- Cycle Life: 500-800 full charge cycles at 80% capacity retention

**Safety Features**
- Over-temperature protection (trips at 65°C)
- Over-current protection (auto-disconnect at 5A)
- Short-circuit protection: Built-in fuse

**Environmental Specifications**
- Operating Temperature Range: 0°C to 45°C
- Storage Temperature Range: -20°C to 35°C
- Humidity Tolerance: 20% to 80% RH (non-condensing)

**Certifications**
- CE Mark (European Conformity)
- FCC ID: 2AOKB-BX3000 (USA)
- RoHS Compliant
- UL Certified

## Warranty

- Standard Warranty: 24 months from date of purchase
- Coverage: Manufacturing defects and premature capacity loss
- Exclusions: Physical damage, unauthorized modification, misuse

## Storage & Handling

- Store in cool, dry place
- Maintain charge level at 50% for long-term storage
- Avoid exposure to extreme temperatures
- Keep away from moisture and corrosive substances
