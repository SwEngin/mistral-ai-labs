## AX-40 equipment maintenance manual

The AX-40 is a fictional air circulation unit. Isolate the power supply before
maintenance and allow moving parts to stop. Inspect the intake grille weekly
and clean it when dust is visible.

| Specification | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Model | AX-40 |
| Rated voltage | 24 V DC |
| Rated current | 2.5 A |
| Airflow | 180 m³/h |
| Operating temperature | 5 °C to 40 °C |
| Filter inspection interval | 30 days |

### Maintenance procedure

1. Switch off and isolate the power supply.
2. Remove the intake cover and inspect the filter.
3. Clean or replace the filter, then reinstall the cover.
4. Restore power and check for unusual noise.

filter, and fan. The scanned PDF reproduces the overview and specifications
